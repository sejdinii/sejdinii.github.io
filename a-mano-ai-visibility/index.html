What we did

We tested the questions real customers could ask before choosing an Italian restaurant in Berlin.

Six customer needs, each tested in English and German.

ChatGPT Search, Google AI Mode, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot.

Answers, access blocks, sources, screenshots, facts and booking links.

What we found

A MANO is visible, but the result changes depending on what the customer asks and which language or platform is used.

What is working

A MANO is frequently recommended. Its strongest position is for customers looking near Strausberger Platz.

What is not working

Only 5 of 96 answers gave people a direct A MANO booking route.

Where visibility is weaker

German questions, Alexanderplatz, handmade pasta and private-event searches were less consistent.

Where trust is weaker

Only 23 of 96 answers showed at least one source clearly about A MANO. Some answers added facts that still need owner confirmation.

3

What is needed

The baseline is complete. Before changing public information, the owner must approve one fact sheet and one official booking route.

Use the completed baseline

The 144-slot pre-change measurement is frozen. It can now be used for the later like-for-like comparison.

Approve facts and booking

The website and public profiles should not be updated until the owner confirms the exact facts below and selects one A MANO-specific reservation destination.

Official business details

Confirm the official name, address, phone number, website and how the location should be described.

Hours, prices and payment

Confirm service hours, kitchen closing time, exceptions, price range and accepted payment methods.

Restaurant features

Confirm outdoor seating, accessibility, takeaway, parking, handmade pasta and private-event options.

How customers book

Choose one official booking link, explain how confirmation works and provide a phone fallback.

Why this matters: AI answers currently mix correct information with unconfirmed claims about prices, hours, accessibility, pasta, private events and other details. One approved fact sheet prevents new contradictions.
4

What we can do to make it better

The work should follow this order so the final before-and-after comparison is fair.

  1. 1
    Confirm the correct facts

    The owner confirms the official name, hours, prices, services, event facts and one booking route.

  2. 2
    Choose one official booking route

    Use one A MANO-specific destination across the website and important profiles.

  3. 3
    Fix the biggest blockers

    Repair the broken booking and menu links, then correct unclear business information.

  4. 4
    Update the website and public profiles

    Make the website, maps and major profiles show the same clear facts in German and English.

  5. 5
    Measure and test again

    Track reservation clicks where possible, then repeat the same questions and compare with this baseline.

What should improve

  • The same A MANO name and business details everywhere.
  • A direct booking route people can use from an AI answer.
  • Stronger German visibility.
  • Clear proof for pasta, business lunch and events.
  • The same facts on the website, maps and directory profiles.
  • A clear before-and-after comparison using the same questions.
Important limitation: AI answers change over time. These results came from real public AI search products on recorded dates. They are the completed starting point before improvements; they do not guarantee the same answer every time or prove booking growth, revenue impact or causal change.
